PACFAS — A5 FOLDED / STAPLED MANUAL
Prepared 17 October 2026

FILES
PACFAS_Manual_A5.pdf: 24 A5 pages in ordinary reading order.
PACFAS_Booklet_A4.pdf: 12 A4 landscape sides, already imposed for a 6-sheet booklet.
PACFAS_Manual_A5_Editable.docx: editable manuscript; Word may reflow pages.
Use the PDFs, not the editable manuscript, for the controlled print layout.

RECOMMENDED: PRINT THE ALREADY-ARRANGED A4 FILE
1. Open PACFAS_Booklet_A4.pdf in a PDF reader (Adobe Reader is suitable).
2. Paper: A4. Orientation: landscape. Scale: Actual size / 100%.
3. Use ONE PDF page per physical side. Choose ordinary printing, NOT Booklet
   and NOT 2 pages per sheet. The two A5 pages are already placed on each side.
4. Enable double-sided printing, FLIP ON SHORT EDGE (landscape).
5. TEST FIRST: print only PDF pages 1–2 as one duplex sheet. Its front should
   read [24 | 1], and its back [2 | 23]. Hold the cover upright and turn the
   sheet left-to-right like a book: the back must also read upright.
6. If the back is upside down, change the duplex flip setting and repeat the
   one-sheet test. Printer driver wording can vary. Do not print all six sheets
   until the fold and orientation are correct.
7. Once correct, print all 12 PDF pages as six duplex sheets. Use normal order,
   no reverse order and no automatic booklet rearrangement. Collate per copy.
8. Nest the sheets with [24 | 1] outermost and [14 | 11] innermost. Fold them
   together at the centre. Page 1 is the front cover; page 24 is the back cover.
9. Crease firmly with a ruler. Saddle-staple at two positions along the fold,
   about 50 mm from the top and bottom. Use a long-reach stapler, or have a
   print shop do the two staples. Supervise any manual staple work carefully.

IF YOUR PRINTER DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY PRINT BOTH SIDES
The required page order does not change, but tray/feed orientation is specific
 to the printer. Test only PDF page 1 (front), reinsert that SAME sheet and
print PDF page 2 (back). Note the successful feed direction. Repeat one sheet
at a time for PDF pages 3/4, 5/6, 7/8, 9/10 and 11/12. This is slower but avoids
assuming whether your printer reverses or flips its output stack.

ALTERNATIVE: LET ADOBE READER DO THE BOOKLET ARRANGEMENT
Use PACFAS_Manual_A5.pdf (NOT the A4 imposed file). Choose Booklet, A4 paper,
left binding, both sides. Test one sheet before printing the full booklet.
Do not mix this method with the already-imposed A4 file.

PAPER AND FINISH
Ordinary A4 paper is sufficient; 80–100 gsm is comfortable for six folded
sheets. Use the same stock for the included cover. No trimming is required.
Margins keep text away from ordinary printer non-printable edges. Colours are
optional: the manual also uses headings, labels and tables for grayscale use.
